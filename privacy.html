Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

In short: FliPlay has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your videos, drawings and analyses stay on your devices. We, the developers, do not receive them.

Who we are

FliPlay ("the app") is a video coaching app for gymnastics and trampoline. Questions about this policy: tumblingdifficultyjudgepro@gmail.com.

What the app uses on your device

  • Camera: to record training and show it with a delay. Recording happens only while you use the camera screens.
  • Microphone: to record sound with videos, and your voice when you record an explanation in the Studio.
  • Photos and videos: only the videos you choose to import, and saving videos or photos you export.
  • Local network: to link your own devices (for example a phone as the camera and a tablet as the screen) and to cast to a TV.
  • Notifications: to tell you when a scan has finished or a live session is running.
  • Location (Android 12 and older only): Android requires this permission to find nearby devices over Wi-Fi Direct. The app does not read or store your location.

Analysis happens on your device

Body tracking, salto and twist counting and angle measurement run entirely on your phone or tablet (using Google's on-device MediaPipe library, with its usage statistics turned off). Videos are not uploaded for analysis.

What leaves your device

  • App updates: when the app starts it checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) for a newer version. The request includes the app version, the platform and a random installation ID generated by the app. This ID is not linked to your name, account or device hardware. Like any internet request it also carries your IP address.
  • Casting to a TV: when you cast, the video goes from your device to your TV over your local network. Google's Cast library sends Google anonymous data about how casting is used and performs, to improve casting.
  • Linking your devices: video travels directly between your devices on your network, encrypted. It does not pass through our servers; we do not have any.
  • Sharing: when you share or export a video or photo, it goes only where you choose.

We do not sell data, we do not use advertising or analytics services, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Storage and deletion

Your library (videos, drawings, scans and settings) is stored only on your device. Delete a clip in the app to remove it, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

Children and people you film

FliPlay is meant for coaches, parents and athletes aged 13 and over, and is not directed at children under 13. If you film other people, especially minors, make sure you have their consent or their parents' consent, and keep the videos private unless you have permission to share them.

Security

Links between your devices are encrypted and need a code shown on your devices. Videos are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
